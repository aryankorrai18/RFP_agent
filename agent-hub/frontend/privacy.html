Privacy

Your data, plainly. What is kept, where it goes, and who sees it.

Agent Hub runs on the machine of the person who set it up. This page describes exactly what it stores, what it sends to outside services, and how one company's data is kept apart from another's.

Last updated 9 October 2026

What is stored, and where

Everything is kept in files on the machine that runs the hub and its agents:

  • Accounts: your email, name, company and role. Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes, and sign-in sessions only as hashes of their tokens.
  • Chats: the messages you send, the files you attach, the agents' replies, and a title taken from your first message.
  • Your company's memory: company facts, sales plays, deals with their emails and notes, RFP projects, drafted and approved answers, reviews and outcomes.
  • Records: an activity log of who changed what (never a password), and counts of model calls and tokens used.

What is sent to outside services

Google Gemini (the language model). The hub sends your chat message to Gemini to understand what you are asking. When an agent does model work (reading a deal, writing a brief, reading an RFP, drafting answers), the relevant notes, documents, company facts and approved answers are sent to Gemini. Agent work always tells you the number of model calls and waits for your confirmation first.

Hindsight Cloud (the memory service), when cloud memory is switched on. Approved answers, deal notes and summaries, and the lessons learned from reviews and outcomes are stored there so the agents can search them. The hub's own files remain the authoritative record, and anything Hindsight returns is checked against them before it is used.

On a free Gemini key, Google's terms for unpaid services may allow it to use submitted content to improve its products and may involve human review. Use a paid key before entering a real customer's data, and only enter information you are allowed to process with these providers.

Who can see what

  • Each company has its own workspaces in every agent. Members see only their own company's data, and only their own chats.
  • Administrators run the hub (companies, people, usage, security). They belong to a separate team that has no access to any company's data.
  • Nothing is shared between companies, and the agents never pool one company's memory with another's.

Cookies and browser storage

One cookie keeps you signed in. It is strictly necessary, sent only to this hub, and unreadable by page scripts. Your browser also remembers your light or dark theme and the chat you had open. There are no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and no third-party scripts beyond the Google Fonts typeface.

How long it is kept

  • Deleting a chat removes it from your history and its link stops working; its records stay on disk for the activity trail.
  • Deleting a company can also permanently delete its data in every agent, when the administrator chooses to.
  • Backups, when made, contain the same data and are kept on this machine.

Security

Keys to outside services stay on the server and never reach the browser. Requests are limited per person, pages refuse injected scripts, cross-site requests are blocked, and the agents can be locked so only the hub can call them. To ask about your data, or to have it corrected or removed, contact the administrator of this hub.
