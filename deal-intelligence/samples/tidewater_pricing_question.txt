Internal CRM note, Marcus Webb, Tidewater Freight (discovery)

Nadia Kowalczyk called this afternoon with a pricing question before she takes anything to her CFO. She asked whether the $95,000 a year figure I mentioned on the first call is per warehouse, per user, or a flat platform fee. I explained that it is a flat platform fee covering up to 15 users and unlimited connectors, with the forecasting module for the 600 lanes included. She then asked what happens if they open a ninth warehouse next spring; I said there is no change in price until they pass 25 users.

She also said that Tidewater's budget for this year is probably around $80,000 and that a three-year term might be needed to get approval. I did not offer a discount and said I would prepare a price summary for her to share with Oliver Grant, the CFO. She mentioned a competitor, Brightline, who had sent a deck last month. I have asked her to send me a copy.
